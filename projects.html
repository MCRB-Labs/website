Focus

An automatic screen-time tracker for Windows. It watches your foreground window, classifies your time as productive, distracted, or neutral, and scores your day 0–100. No manual timers.

  • Fully automatic classification
  • Local-only storage, nothing leaves your machine
  • History, goals, leaderboard, and share views

Hardware · Concept

Focus USB Hub

A physical USB hub that takes instructions from the Focus app. Cross your screen-time line for the day, and it can throttle or cut power to connected devices until you're back on track.

  • Controlled directly by your Focus Score
  • Configurable per-port rules
  • Early concept, details coming soon
