How it works

Set it up once. It runs itself.

Three quiet steps, no daily input required.

01

It tracks in the background

Focus watches your active foreground window — the app or browser tab actually in front of you — without interrupting anything.

02

It classifies each app and site

Every app and site is sorted into productive, distracted, or neutral, using rules you can see and edit at any time.

03

You get a score and a streak

At a glance: a 0–100 score for the day, and a running streak that rewards consistency, not just good days.

At a glance

The four numbers that matter

Pulled straight from the app's own Today view.

Focus Score

A single 0–100 number for your day, weighted by how you actually spent your time.

Day Streak

Consecutive days you've hit your target score — a reason to show up again tomorrow.

Productive Time

Total hours logged in apps and sites you've classified as productive today.

Distracted Time

Where the day actually went — tracked plainly, with no judgment attached.

Smart detection

The details that make it trustworthy

Two things most trackers get wrong — that Focus handles by default.

Idle detection that understands audio

Most trackers mark you "away" the moment your mouse stops moving — flagging a silently-watched lecture or video as idle. Focus checks whether audio is actually playing before it calls you away, so a video you're genuinely watching stays counted.

Sites aren't all-or-nothing. Mark just the educational half of a YouTube session as productive, independent of the general site rule — without reclassifying every video you'll ever watch there.

Privacy

Everything stays on your machine.

Focus logs activity to a local SQLite database on your computer. There's no account, no server, and no sync — nothing about what you do ever leaves your machine.
