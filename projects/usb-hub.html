A USB hub that listens to your focus score.

The Focus USB Hub is a small piece of desk hardware controlled directly by the Focus app. Go over your screen-time line, and it can throttle or stop charging the devices plugged into it, automatically, with no app-switching required.

How it works with Focus

Your focus score becomes a physical signal

The hub doesn't make its own decisions. It just reacts to the same score you already see in the app.

01

Focus tracks your day

The Focus app keeps classifying your time and updating your score exactly as it does today.

02

You set the rule

Choose a trigger (a distracted-time cap, a minimum score, or a specific time of day) and what should happen when it's crossed.

03

The hub responds instantly

Connected over USB to your PC, the hub throttles or cuts charging on chosen ports the moment your rule is triggered, and restores it automatically once you're back under the line.

Planned features

What we're designing toward

Subject to change as the hardware moves from concept to prototype.

Per-port control

Set different rules for different ports: throttle a phone, leave a work laptop untouched.

Screen-time triggers

Trigger on distracted-time caps, a minimum score, or time-of-day windows you define in Focus.

Manual override

A physical or in-app override for when you genuinely need power, no questions asked.

Local pairing only

Talks to the Focus app on the same machine over USB, with no cloud pairing and no app store account.

We'll share prototypes and specs as the hub moves out of the concept stage.
