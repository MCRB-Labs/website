About

Two students, building the tools we wanted to use.

MCRB Labs started as a shared frustration with productivity apps that ask for more effort than they save. We're building the opposite: tools that watch, quietly, and tell you the truth.

What we believe

The principles behind every decision

These shape what we build and what we say no to.

01

Automatic, not effortful

If a tool needs daily upkeep to stay useful, it's not done yet. Everything we build should work without being managed.

02

Local by default

Your activity data is yours. We build local-first, and we're explicit any time that ever changes.

03

Honest, not gamified

No streak guilt-trips, no manipulative notifications. A number should tell you the truth, not sell you a subscription.

Our story

How MCRB Labs started

Still early. This timeline will keep growing.

2025

The idea

Frustrated with productivity apps that required more manual tracking than the focus they were meant to save, we started sketching what an automatic version could look like.

2025–26

Building Focus

Started building the Focus app for Windows (foreground-window tracking, classification rules, and the scoring system) alongside A-levels.

2026

MCRB Labs, and the USB Hub concept

Formed MCRB Labs and started designing the Focus USB Hub, hardware that reacts to your Focus Score directly. Both projects are in active development.
