Privacy Policy

How MCRB Labs collects, uses, and protects information across our website and all of our products and services.

Last updated: September 12, 2026.

Overview

MCRB Labs ("MCRB Labs," "we," "us," or "our") builds software and hardware products. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to this website and to any current or future MCRB Labs product or service (collectively, the "Services"), except where a specific product provides additional or different terms.

Information we may collect

  • Contact information you give us directly, such as your name and email address when you get in touch or sign up for updates.
  • Information generated by your use of a Service, which may include account information (where a Service offers accounts), device and technical information, usage and activity data, and content or settings you create within a Service.
  • Information collected automatically through our website, such as standard server logs, and, if we introduce them in the future, analytics or cookies.

Not every Service collects every category above. What a specific product collects depends on how that product works, and may be described in more detail within that product or its own documentation.

How we use information

  • To operate, provide, maintain, and improve our Services.
  • To communicate with you, including responding to inquiries and sending updates you've requested.
  • To provide accounts, syncing, or other online functionality where a Service offers them.
  • To comply with legal obligations and to protect the rights, property, and safety of MCRB Labs, our users, and the public.

How information is stored

Some of our Services may be designed to process and store information locally on your device. Others may use accounts, syncing, or remote servers to provide their functionality, and we may introduce account-based features to existing or future Services at any time. Where a Service uses an account or server-based storage, that will be disclosed within the Service or its accompanying documentation. We take reasonable measures to protect information in our control, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Sharing of information

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We may share information with service providers who help us operate our Services or business, under obligations to protect that information.
  • We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or government request, or where we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, our users, or the public.
  • Information may be disclosed in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of some or all of our assets.

Third-party services

Our website and Services may link to, or integrate with, third-party services (for example, social media platforms). Those third parties have their own privacy practices, and we are not responsible for how they handle your information.

Children's privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under the age at which local law permits them to consent to data processing without parental involvement, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from such children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

Your choices

You can contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, or to request that we correct or delete it, subject to any legal obligations that may require us to retain certain information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our Services and legal obligations evolve. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of our website or any Service after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the Contact page.
