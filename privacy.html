How MCRB Labs handles data across Fragment and the Fragment USB Hub.

Last updated: Not yet published. Final review pending before public launch.

Overview

MCRB Labs builds Fragment, a Windows app, and the Fragment USB Hub, a piece of hardware controlled by Fragment. This policy explains what data each product touches and where it goes.

Fragment (the app)

Fragment classifies your foreground app and website activity and stores the results locally in a SQLite database on your own machine. Specifically:

  • Activity data (which app or site was in the foreground, and when) never leaves your device.
  • There is no account system and no server-side storage of your activity.
  • Classification rules and settings are stored locally alongside your activity data.

Fragment USB Hub

The USB Hub is a concept-stage product. It is designed to communicate with the Fragment app over a direct USB connection on the same machine, without any cloud pairing or account requirement. This section will be expanded with full detail once the hardware reaches a testable prototype.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your activity data. There's no server for it to sit on in the first place.
  • We don't track you across other websites or apps.
  • We don't require an account to use Fragment.

This website

This marketing site does not currently use analytics or tracking cookies. If that changes, this section will be updated to disclose exactly what's collected and why.
